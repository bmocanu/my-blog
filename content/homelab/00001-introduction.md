I am starting this series of posts about homelab with the goal of sharing my experience in building a homelab tailored to my needs and my ideas for testing and playing around with tech. 

The posts are, somehow, organized logically, from an initial starting point, getting the hardware, deciding on the software, planning how the homelab should look like and work, then moving to updating nodes, backing them up and reaching other more specific and niche topics like building certain types of clusters, hosting your own Certificate Authority and other topics.

There are many ways to build a homelab. This series presents my opinionated way, what worked for me inside the constraints of money, time and ideas that I had. 

Not all the parts of this series will be available right away, as I like to plan ahead, add the pages in the list, and fill them in once I find the time.

Enjoy!
